Wikipe-tan is the Wikipedia/Wikimedia anime mascot, released by its artists under free licenses.

The second batch (`clean-*` and `messy-*`) is sorted by how hard the picture is for a tracer: `clean-*` has flat cel colors and clear lines, `messy-*` has painterly or soft coloring. None was edited except that files larger than 2000 px on the longest side were downscaled (noted above); Pepper&Carrot is by David Revoy, https://www.peppercarrot.com. Arch-tan's Commons page lists a Pixiv link only as the place the artist first posted it; the CC BY 4.0 license is stated on the Commons file page.
